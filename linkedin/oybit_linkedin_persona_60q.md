# Oybit Persona Engine — LinkedIn (60 Questions)

> **Purpose:** Extract enough signal from a user to build a `persona.md` file that makes AI-generated LinkedIn content sound like them — their voice, their ideas, their goals — not a generic professional.
>
> **Structure:** 7 extraction layers × 60 questions. Each question includes a `→ Why` annotation for the prompt builder.
>
> **Delivery:** Core 10 at onboarding. Remaining 50 unlock progressively as the user posts and engages.

---

## Layer 01 — Who You Are
*Professional identity extraction. How you see yourself and how the world should see you.*

**Q1.** What's your professional title or role, and how do you want people to introduce you?
→ *Sets the anchor for every post's authorial voice.*

**Q2.** How many years of experience do you have in your field?
→ *Calibrates authority level and how you frame expertise.*

**Q3.** Name the 3 biggest professional wins you've had in the last 2 years.
→ *Source material for proof-driven storytelling.*

**Q4.** What problem do you solve for the world? One sentence.
→ *The core positioning statement that flavors all content.*

**Q5.** How did you get into your field — what's the origin story?
→ *The "why I'm here" narrative for authenticity posts.*

**Q6.** Are you a solo founder, part of a team, or building something on the side?
→ *Frames the builder angle and content perspective.*

**Q7.** What companies, institutions, or brands have shaped your professional identity?
→ *Social proof signals and affiliation framing.*

**Q8.** Do you primarily lead people, build products, sell things, or create ideas?
→ *Determines which content archetypes fit you naturally.*

**Q9.** What's the thing you know that most people in your space genuinely don't?
→ *The "secret insight" that makes your POV worth following.*

**Q10.** If someone followed you on LinkedIn for 3 months, what would they say you're known for?
→ *The intended brand impression — quality check on the full persona.*

---

## Layer 02 — Voice & Tone
*How you sound. The stylistic fingerprint that makes content recognizably yours.*

**Q11.** Pick 3 adjectives that describe exactly how you want to sound on LinkedIn.
→ *Primary tone parameters for the AI prompt builder.*

**Q12.** Are you more formal & authoritative, or casual & relatable — or somewhere in between?
→ *Baseline register calibration.*

**Q13.** Do you use humor? If yes — dry wit, self-deprecating, playful, or sharp?
→ *Humor type affects sentence structure and timing.*

**Q14.** How do you feel about using slang or internet language in professional posts?
→ *Code-switching threshold.*

**Q15.** How long do your sentences naturally run — short punchy lines or longer structured paragraphs?
→ *Rhythm fingerprint — controls formatting decisions.*

**Q16.** Do you prefer telling stories or sharing frameworks?
→ *Narrative vs. analytical content split.*

**Q17.** When someone reads your post, what emotion do you want them to feel?
→ *Emotional intent shapes word choice and pacing.*

**Q18.** Name 2–3 LinkedIn creators whose writing style you admire — and why.
→ *Style reference anchors for pattern matching.*

**Q19.** What LinkedIn writing habits genuinely annoy you — buzzwords, phrases, styles?
→ *The negative voice space — what Oybit must avoid for you.*

**Q20.** Do you write in first person naturally, or does it feel uncomfortable to you?
→ *Affects how direct and personal the content can be.*

---

## Layer 03 — Content Pillars
*What you actually talk about. The intellectual raw material for all generated content.*

**Q21.** List 5 topics you could talk about for hours without notes.
→ *Primary content pillars — the main topic rotation.*

**Q22.** What's the most controversial opinion you hold in your industry?
→ *Hot-take and debate-starter content source.*

**Q23.** What do most people in your field get completely wrong?
→ *The "myth-buster" angle that drives high engagement.*

**Q24.** What topic do you wish more people talked about in your niche?
→ *Underserved angle for thought leadership differentiation.*

**Q25.** What recent experience — win, loss, or lesson — do you want to write about?
→ *Timely story content that feels current, not evergreen.*

**Q26.** Are you currently building something? What's update-worthy about where you are now?
→ *Build-in-public material and proof of motion.*

**Q27.** What tools, workflows, or systems have genuinely changed how you work?
→ *Tactical recommendation content — high saves, high shares.*

**Q28.** What books, podcasts, or ideas have shaped your thinking this year?
→ *Intellectual influence mapping for associative credibility.*

**Q29.** What mistakes have you made that others in your space could learn from?
→ *Vulnerability-based content — strongest trust signal on LinkedIn.*

**Q30.** What advice do you give people who are 2–3 years behind you in your field?
→ *Mentorship-tone posts — drives followers and comments.*

---

## Layer 04 — Your Audience
*Who you're writing to. Shapes framing, vocabulary, and the assumed baseline.*

**Q31.** Who do you most want to reach — job title, industry, career stage?
→ *The primary reader avatar for every content decision.*

**Q32.** Are you writing for beginners, intermediate practitioners, or peer-level experts?
→ *Jargon threshold and explanation depth calibration.*

**Q33.** What is your audience's biggest professional frustration right now?
→ *Empathy hook material — "I know what you're dealing with" posts.*

**Q34.** What does your audience want but doesn't know how to ask for?
→ *The "aha moment" content angle — reads as insight, not advice.*

**Q35.** What type of LinkedIn content does your audience already engage with?
→ *Existing behavior baseline — don't fight the format.*

**Q36.** What's the biggest misconception your audience has about your field or what you do?
→ *Correction-narrative content — authority-builder.*

**Q37.** Would you describe your ideal reader as skeptical, aspirational, or a peer?
→ *Determines whether to prove, inspire, or challenge.*

**Q38.** What kind of person do you NOT want to attract on LinkedIn?
→ *Anti-audience signals — helps Oybit avoid the wrong framing.*

---

## Layer 05 — Story & Narrative
*The deeper narrative layer. What makes your journey worth following.*

**Q39.** What's the hardest professional moment you've had, and what did it teach you?
→ *The core adversity story — strongest performing content type on LinkedIn.*

**Q40.** What's a clear before-and-after from your career that people should know?
→ *Transformation arc — drives comments and saves.*

**Q41.** Is there a moment you almost quit or pivoted significantly? What happened?
→ *Tension and resolution narrative — high relatability.*

**Q42.** What's something personal about you that genuinely shapes how you work?
→ *Character humanization — makes the professional voice feel real.*

**Q43.** What do people always ask you about at networking events or in DMs?
→ *Social proof that validates your expertise positioning.*

**Q44.** If you wrote a book right now, what would the title be?
→ *Crystallizes your intellectual brand in a single phrase.*

**Q45.** What belief do you hold that used to be unpopular but is now going mainstream?
→ *"I said it first" content — positions you as ahead of the curve.*

**Q46.** What legacy do you want to leave in your field in 10 years?
→ *Vision framing for mission-driven content that builds community.*

---

## Layer 06 — Engagement Style
*How you operate on the platform. Behavior signals that shape the posting strategy.*

**Q47.** Do you reply to every comment, most comments, or let conversations run naturally?
→ *Engagement rate targeting and response automation scope.*

**Q48.** How do you handle pushback or disagreement in your comments?
→ *Conflict response tone — lean in, deflect, or stay neutral.*

**Q49.** Do you tag companies or people in posts, or keep it purely idea-driven?
→ *Tagging strategy affects reach and political risk.*

**Q50.** Do you want to post on a consistent schedule or only when something's worth saying?
→ *Posting cadence and batch vs. reactive mode.*

**Q51.** How personal are you willing to get — strictly professional or share life context too?
→ *Personal disclosure threshold — affects warmth and relatability.*

**Q52.** Do you want to reshare and comment on others' posts as part of your presence?
→ *Network activation vs. pure broadcast approach.*

**Q53.** How do you feel about polls, open questions, or obvious engagement prompts?
→ *Engagement mechanics permission scope.*

**Q54.** What's one type of post you would never make on LinkedIn — and why?
→ *Hard content boundary for the negative constraint layer.*

---

## Layer 07 — Goals
*What success looks like. Grounds the content strategy in actual outcomes.*

**Q55.** What do you want LinkedIn to do for you — leads, hiring, brand, community, or investors?
→ *Primary objective — determines content-to-CTA ratio and framing.*

**Q56.** What's your current follower count and where do you want it in 12 months?
→ *Growth target for scheduling cadence and volume decisions.*

**Q57.** Are you optimizing for likes and comments, profile views, DMs, or off-platform conversions?
→ *Success metric calibration for the feedback loop.*

**Q58.** What doors do you specifically want LinkedIn to open for you this year?
→ *Concrete outcome framing — keeps content purposeful.*

**Q59.** How much time per week can you realistically put into LinkedIn content?
→ *Workload constraint — sets automation depth requirement.*

**Q60.** What does "LinkedIn working" actually look like for you? Name the specific signal.
→ *The north star signal that Oybit's analytics loop optimizes toward.*

---

## Notes for the Prompt Builder

- **Q1–Q10** are asked at onboarding (core 30 across all platforms; LinkedIn gets Q1–Q10 here)
- **Q11–Q30** unlock after the user's first 3 posts
- **Q31–Q60** unlock progressively over 30 days or when engagement data starts coming in
- Every answer maps to a field in `persona.md` under the `linkedin` platform voice modifier block
- Q19 and Q54 feed directly into the **negative constraint layer** — these are as important as the positive signals
- Q55 + Q60 together define the feedback loop's north star for the LinkedIn analytics module
