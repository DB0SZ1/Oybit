## THE STRUCTURAL REALITY OF FACEBOOK PAGES IN 2026
Facebook Pages have significantly lower organic reach than personal profiles — averaging **1.52% of followers** reached per post. This is structural.

**What this means for C0DE's page:**
- The page is primarily a **credibility anchor** — it exists so that when someone searches "C0DE software Abuja" they find a professional, active page
- Organic reach is supplemented by Idris's personal profile sharing page posts
- **Facebook Reels on Pages** get significantly more reach than static posts — meme posts in Reel format punch through the Page reach ceiling
- Paid ads ($5–$10/day targeting Abuja/Lagos SME owners) are more effective ROI than posting frequency

## FACEBOOK PAGE ALGORITHM — SPECIFIC RULES

### What Drives Page Reach
1. **Reels format** — 135% more reach than static images. Meme posts in Reel format are the highest organic reach opportunity for the page.
2. **Comment replies within 6 hours** — Facebook signals "active page" and boosts ranking.
3. **Shares from personal profiles** — When Idris shares the page's post on his personal profile, it gains personal-profile-level distribution.
4. **External links in post body = reach penalty** — WhatsApp link in the first comment only.
5. **Facebook Groups crossposting** — Share page posts into relevant Abuja business groups.
6. **Page completeness** — Every profile field must be filled.

### Page-Specific Ranking Factors
- **Recency:** Consistent posting ranks higher than bursts
- **Engagement rate per post:** 3–5 high-engagement posts/week beats 10 low-engagement posts
- **Response rate:** Fast response time improves credibility and reach
- **Reviews:** 4+ stars from 5+ reviews = visible trust badge

## VOICE GUIDE

### Tone
Professional, reliable, local-aware, results-focused. Warmer than a corporate agency. Like a well-run small business that knows exactly what it does.

### Opening Rules

| ✅ Works | ❌ Doesn't Work |
|---|---|
| "Does your Abuja business have a website that actually brings in clients?" | "We are excited to announce our services..." |
| "Here is what we just shipped for a client in Lagos." | "C0DE is a world-class software agency..." |
| "5 signs your website is costing you money right now." | "Please like and share our page!" |

### Caption Length Rules
- **Meme posts:** 1–2 line caption. Let the video land. Include soft CTA.
- **Offer posts:** 30–60 words.
- **Testimonial posts:** 80–150 words.
- **Educational posts:** 200–350 words.
- **Portfolio posts:** 50–100 words.

### Never Say
- "We are the best / leading / top agency in Nigeria"
- "Cheap" or "affordable"
- "Please like and share"
- "We are excited / thrilled / proud to announce"
- Technical jargon without business translation
