## WHY LINKEDIN IS STRUCTURALLY DIFFERENT

### Personal Profiles vs Company Pages
LinkedIn personal profiles generate **8x more engagement** than company pages. Idris's personal profile is the primary LinkedIn asset.

### The Golden Window
The first **60–90 minutes** after posting determine total reach. Every post must be seeded immediately — DM 3–5 relevant connections after posting to trigger early engagement velocity.

### The External Link Penalty
Posts with external links in the body receive **40% less initial reach.**

**Rule:** Never put a URL in the post body. Publish the post, wait for 10+ reactions, then add the link in the **first comment.**

### The "I" Penalty
Posts beginning with "I" perform worse consistently.

**Rule:** No post on this profile begins with the word "I."

## LINKEDIN ALGORITHM — FULL RULES

### Content Ranking Signals (Priority Order)
1. **Early engagement velocity** — Reactions + comments in first 60–90 minutes determine distribution tier
2. **Comment depth** — A 3-sentence comment counts more than "Great post!"
3. **Saves** — Carousel/educational posts earn saves
4. **Dwell time** — Long-form text with visual breaks earns more dwell time
5. **Profile visit rate** — Viewers who tap through to the profile after seeing a post
6. **Shares** — Reposting with commentary amplifies reach

### Format Performance (Verified 2026)
| Format | Performance | Best Use |
|---|---|---|
| Native Carousels (PDF upload) | **Highest impressions — 3x vs static image** | Deep dives, step-by-step, case studies |
| Text posts (strong hook + narrative) | **High engagement velocity** | Founder stories, opinions, lessons |
| Native video | Growing; performs well under 3 minutes | Meme posts, product demos |
| Polls | Spikes engagement; use max 2–3x/month | Quick opinions, market research |
| Single image | Moderate | Supporting a text post |
| Link posts | **Lowest reach** | Never in post body — first comment only |

### The Carousel Rule
LinkedIn carousels must be uploaded as **PDF files** — not image carousels. 8–10 slides is the sweet spot.
- Slide 1: The entire argument in one bold sentence
- Slides 2–8: One idea per slide
- Last slide: Specific CTA + Idris's name/handle

### Commenting Strategy (Supplementary Growth)
5 high-quality comments per day on posts from founders with 5K+ followers. Each comment must be 3+ sentences adding a real perspective. Never generic.

## VOICE GUIDE

### Tone
Intellectual, data-grounded, quietly authoritative. The voice of a founder who has shipped real things and can discuss them with precision. Not arrogant. Not hustle culture.

### Opening Rules (Non-Negotiable)
**NEVER start a post with "I".**

| ✅ Works | ❌ Doesn't Work |
|---|---|
| "Most founders over-engineer their first SaaS." | "I am a founder who builds..." |
| "18 months of building in Abuja taught me one thing:" | "I'm excited to share that..." |
| "ColdSift hit [X] users last week. Here's what changed." | "As a young Nigerian developer..." |

### The "See More" Hook
Line 1: The hook
Line 2: The tension or contrast
Line 3: The promise of what clicking "see more" delivers

### Closing Rules
- A specific question: "What's been the hardest part of your first build?"
- A direct CTA: "DM me 'MVP' if you're working on something similar."
- A statement that invites disagreement: "Disagree? I want to hear it."

### Never Say
- "I'm just a student"
- "Please check out my work"
- Hustle-culture buzzwords
- "Kindly"
