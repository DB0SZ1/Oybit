## FACEBOOK ALGORITHM — PERSONAL PROFILE RULES

### What Drives Reach
1. **Comments** — Real replies outrank likes. Posts that start conversations rank higher. Ask questions.
2. **Shares** — Someone sharing your post = highest distribution signal. Memes and story content earn shares.
3. **Saves** — Educational or long-form content earns saves.
4. **Reactions** — All reactions count more than a simple like.
5. **Comment replies within 6 hours** — Reply to comments on your own posts within 6 hours. Facebook reads it as active discussion and boosts the post's rank.

### Critical Rules
- **External links kill reach** — Never put URLs in the post body. Add the link in the **first comment** after posting.
- **Reels get 135% more reach** than static photos on Facebook — meme Reels benefit from this
- **Never use engagement bait** — "Like and share this post" gets suppressed. Earn it through content.

### Pillar 2 — Local Business Education
Posts that explain the value of digital tools to non-technical Abuja/Lagos business owners. No jargon. Pure business outcome framing.

### Pillar 3 — Testimonials (The Primary Conversion Weapon)
One WhatsApp/DM screenshot per 5-post cycle, with Idris's context written around it.

**Why it works:** Raw WhatsApp screenshot authenticity bypasses Nigerian buyer scepticism faster than any polished testimonial graphic.

## VOICE GUIDE

### Tone
Warm, local, hardworking, humble but quietly confident. The "local hero" who made it but still feels like one of us. Not corporate. Not imported.

### Opening Rules

| ✅ Works | ❌ Doesn't Work |
|---|---|
| "Sitting in a cafe in Wuse 2 right now, working on this..." | "I am pleased to announce..." |
| "Something happened this week that I have to share." | "As a young entrepreneur, I've learned..." |
| "Alhamdulillah — big week." | "Exciting news from C0DE!" |
| "A client DM'd me this morning and it made my day." | "We at C0DE are proud to present..." |

### Closing Rules
End posts with:
- A genuine question: "Any Abuja business owners facing this? Drop a comment."
- A soft CTA: "WhatsApp me if you need this — link in the first comment."

### Caption Length Rules
- **Meme posts:** Caption is short — 1–2 lines max. Let the video land.
- **Promotional posts:** 50–100 words max.
- **Testimonial posts:** 100–200 words.
- **Story/personal narrative:** 400–800 words.
- **Educational posts:** 200–400 words.

### Never Say
- Corporate language: "leverage," "synergy," "world-class solutions"
- "Please follow / share / like"
- Price-minimising language: "We are very affordable"
- Anything that sounds like it was written by an AI trained on American LinkedIn posts
