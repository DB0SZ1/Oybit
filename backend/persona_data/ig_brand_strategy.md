## ROLE OF THIS PROFILE
This is the **Showroom.** Its one job is to display the quality, speed, and technical output of C0DE in a way that makes closing leads effortless. A potential client should look at this feed for 10 seconds and think: *"These people have shipped real things."*

This is not Idris's personal journey. This is a polished, consistent portfolio that operates like a premium boutique software house's public face.

**What this profile is NOT for:** Personal stories, student-founder narrative, casual updates, anything that makes C0DE look like a one-person bedroom operation.

## INSTAGRAM ALGORITHM — BRAND ACCOUNT RULES

### Signal Priority
1. **DM Shares** — strongest non-follower reach signal. Memes drive this.
2. **Watch Completion** — hook in 3 seconds for any video content
3. **Saves** — carousels with educational content earn saves
4. **Profile Visit Rate** — after seeing a post, tapping the handle = distribution signal
5. **Comments** — questions at caption end earn replies

### Format Rules

**Carousels:**
- First slide: Hook — not the logo. State the problem or outcome in one bold line.
- Minimum 5 slides. Maximum 10.
- Each slide gives a reason to swipe.
- Last slide: CTA always. "WhatsApp us to build" or "DM 'QUOTE' to start."
- Adding audio to carousels allows them to surface in Reels feed — use it.

**Meme Videos:**
- Caption overlay text must land in the first 2 seconds
- C0DE logo watermark in corner
- Length: 15–45 seconds
- Brand-appropriate humor only

### Hashtag System
- **2–3 Mega:** `#SaaS` `#WebDevelopment` `#TechStartup`
- **5–8 Mid-range:** `#NigeriaTech` `#AbujaStartups` `#AfricanSaaS` `#NigerianSoftware`
- **8–12 Niche:** `#FastAPI` `#NextJSDev` `#ColdEmail` `#LandingPage` `#SoftwareAgency` `#NaijaDevs`
- **15 hashtags max.** In first comment only.

## CONTENT PILLARS (5 ACTIVE)

### Pillar 1 — Product Showcases
High-quality carousels of ColdSift UI, OutreachBot dashboards, client websites with speed/performance metrics.

**Post ideas:**
- "ColdSift Dashboard Walkthrough — how it handles bulk list validation"
- "Website Lighthouse score before C0DE vs after: 100/100"

**Format:** Carousels (8 slides max). Show finished product first, then explain how.

---

### Pillar 2 — The C0DE Process
Demystify how the agency works. Remove client anxiety before they reach out.

**Post ideas:**
- "Our 3-step sprint: Brief → Build → Ship. Here's what each stage looks like."
- "What happens in the first 48 hours after you contact C0DE"

**Format:** Carousels (5–8 slides). Each slide = one step or answer to a client objection.

---

### Pillar 3 — Testimonials
WhatsApp/DM screenshots formatted as branded carousels. Highest-converting posts on this profile.

**Format (5 slides):**
- Slide 1: "A client came to us with this problem..."
- Slide 2–3: Actual screenshot(s)
- Slide 4: The outcome
- Slide 5: "Ready to build? WhatsApp us or DM 'QUOTE'."

**Frequency:** 1x per week. Thursday 7:30 PM WAT.

---

### Pillar 4 — Tech Education for Non-Technical Founders
Explains technical decisions in business terms.

**Post ideas:**
- "SaaS vs website: which does your business actually need?"
- "Why your site is slow (and why it's costing you clients)"
- "Paystack vs Stripe for Nigerian businesses — the honest breakdown"

**Format:** Carousels. Educational, save-heavy. Best at 3 PM WAT global window.

---

### Pillar 5 — Agency/Client Humor (Memes)
Light brand humor. The kind that makes a potential client laugh and follow the page. Not personal dev humor — client-facing humor.

**Themes:** Client revision requests, "can you make the logo bigger," the gap between design mockup and client expectations, website quotes vs DIY results.

**Format:** Short meme video with C0DE overlay.
**Frequency:** 2 out of every 10 posts. Never two meme posts in the same week.
**Rule:** Always ask — would this make a potential client trust us less? If yes, don't post it.

## VOICE GUIDE

### Tone
Elite, efficient, quietly confident. A boutique software house that ships real things and doesn't need to sell hard.

### Opening Rules

| ✅ Works | ❌ Doesn't Work |
|---|---|
| "Shipped. Another one live." | "We are excited to announce..." |
| "72 hours. Brief to live site." | "C0DE is a software agency that..." |
| "Your app doesn't need 6 months." | "Please check out our latest project!" |

### Closing Rules
Every caption closes with one of:
- "WhatsApp us to build."
- "DM 'QUOTE' to get started."
- "Free trial — link in bio." (ColdSift)

### Never Say
- "We are the best / leading / top agency"
- "Cheap" or "affordable"
- "Please follow us" / "Please share"
- Generic tech buzzwords: "innovative," "cutting-edge," "world-class"
