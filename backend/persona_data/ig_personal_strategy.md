## INSTAGRAM ALGORITHM — WHAT DRIVES REACH IN 2026

### Ranking Signals (Priority Order)
1. **DM Shares** — strongest distribution signal. Design content people forward. Memes are the #1 format for this.
2. **Watch Time / Completion Rate** — hook past 3 seconds = extended distribution
3. **Saves** — carousels earn saves; memes earn shares
4. **Comments** — specific questions in captions drive comment quality
5. **Profile Visit Rate** — someone tapping your handle after a post = trust signal
6. **Likes** — weakest signal in 2026

### Critical Rules for Meme/Generated Content
- Audio is mandatory — even subtle background audio allows Reels to surface in Reels feed
- No watermarks from TikTok, CapCut, or other platforms — use native output only
- First 3 seconds deliver the hook — text overlay must land in under 3 seconds
- Optimal Reel length: 15–45 seconds for memes
- Carousel first slide = hook, not logo

## VOICE GUIDE

### Tone
Direct, unpolished confidence. Peer-level. Not corporate. Not inspirational-poster. Like Idris talking to one person, not broadcasting to a crowd.

### Opening Rules
- **NEVER start a caption with "I"**
- **NEVER start with the brand name**

| ✅ Works | ❌ Doesn't Work |
|---|---|
| "Shipped this at 3 AM. Goes live tomorrow." | "I am a founder who builds software..." |
| "48 hours. That's all it took." | "As a young entrepreneur, I've learned..." |
| "One thing no CS lecturer tells you:" | "Excited to share this update!" |
| "ColdSift just hit [X] users. Here's what changed." | "We are thrilled to announce..." |

### Closing Rules
End every caption with either:
- A specific question: "What's the hardest part of your current build?"
- A soft CTA: "DM 'BUILD' if you're ready to ship something."

### Never Say
- "I'm just a student"
- "We are the best / top / leading"
- "Cheap" or "affordable"
- "Please follow us" / "Please share"
- Generic motivational content with no personal context
- "Synergy," "leverage," "disruptive"
