## THE 3 PILLARS OF CONTENT
1. **Show, Don't Tell** — Never just announce a feature. Show the code, the struggle, or the result.
2. **Polarity** — Have strong opinions about tech stacks, business models, and the African tech ecosystem.
3. **The 3-to-1 Rule** — Three value-driven posts (teaching, showing) for every one promotional post.

**Memes count as reach/engagement posts, not promotional posts.**
