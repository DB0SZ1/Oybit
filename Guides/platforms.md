# Oybit — Platform Strategies

> Detailed per-account content strategy, growth playbook, format guide, and posting cadence. Scoped to 4 accounts across 3 platforms.

---

## Instagram — Personal Account (Ahmad)

**Goal:** 3,000–5,000 organic followers by end of 2026
**Voice:** Casual, raw, grind-mode, relatable. Ahmad the person — not Ahmad the brand.
**Core angle:** "18yo in Abuja building real software products solo, no team, no funding. This is what it actually looks like."

**What the algorithm rewards on Instagram:**
- Reels with strong hooks in the first 1–2 seconds
- Carousels that get swipes (each slide must earn the next)
- Saves (strongest signal — content people want to return to)
- Shares (DM shares especially — second strongest signal)
- Comments that are more than one word
- Consistent posting cadence (algorithm punishes gaps)

**Ahmad's angle meets the algorithm:**
- Transformation content: "Before I built Oybit vs after" — algorithm loves transformation, Ahmad's story is genuinely transformative
- Build-in-public: real product screenshots, real code, real failures — Instagram audiences love authenticity over polish
- Trend adaptation: personal IG specifically adapts to trending audio, trending formats, trending developer/founder discussions. MiroFish surfaces what's trending in Ahmad's niche and Oybit adapts the format while keeping Ahmad's voice
- Nigerian/Abuja angle: underrepresented perspective in tech content — differentiation by geography and background is a natural hook

**Content formats:**
| Format | Frequency | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Reels (Remotion) | 4–5x/week | 15–30s, clean motion graphics, kinetic text hook, brand colors |
| Carousels | 3x/week | 5–8 slides, cover headline → story → lesson → CTA |
| Single image | 2x/week | Product screenshot, milestone, behind-the-scenes |
| Stories | Daily | Casual, polls, questions, behind-the-scenes — no Remotion needed |

**Carousel structure for personal IG:**
```
Slide 1: Hook — one punchy sentence that creates tension or curiosity
Slide 2: The situation — what actually happened
Slide 3: What I discovered / what went wrong
Slide 4: The system insight — what this reveals
Slide 5: The lesson / constraint
Slide 6: Relatable framing — why YOU should care
Slide 7: (Optional) CTA — follow for more of this
```

**Reel script structure (Remotion):**
```
0–2s: Text hook on screen + trending audio
2–8s: The situation (fast cuts, text overlays)
8–18s: The insight/reveal
18–25s: The lesson in one line
25–30s: Follow CTA
```

**Content pillars for personal IG (with weights):**
- Building in public (30%) — real product work, real decisions
- African founder perspective (25%) — Abuja, Nigeria, African tech
- Technical storytelling (25%) — systems, code, architecture — made human
- Personal grind moments (20%) — 2AM builds, shipping days, real reactions

**Hard stops for personal IG:**
- No engagement bait ("comment YES if you agree")
- No reposting other people's content without heavy original commentary
- No vague announcements without specifics
- No content that could embarrass Nyvora as a company

**Trend adaptation rules:**
- Audio trends: match trending audio when the content fits. Never force it.
- Format trends: if a format is viral (e.g. "POV" series, "rate my X"), adapt it with Ahmad's angle
- Topic trends: MiroFish surfaces rising narratives. Only post on trends that pass Content DNA check

**Hashtag strategy:**
Mix of 3 tiers per post:
- Tier 1 (high volume, competitive): #indiehacker #buildinpublic #developer
- Tier 2 (medium, niche): #africatech #nigeriatech #abujastartup
- Tier 3 (low volume, owned): #nyvora #oybit (when relevant)
Max 8–10 hashtags. Never more.

## LinkedIn — Ahmad's Profile

**Goal:** 3,000–5,000 organic followers by end of 2026
**Voice:** Systems thinker, technical founder, professional but real. Lessons earned not borrowed.
**Core angle:** "Young African technical founder. Shipped real products. Here's what I've actually learned."

**What the algorithm rewards on LinkedIn:**
- Text posts that get early comments (first hour is critical)
- Posts that keep people reading (LinkedIn measures dwell time)
- Saves and reshares from high-follower accounts
- Consistent posting (5x/week minimum to stay in algorithm)
- Posts that generate discussion in comments

**Ahmad's proven winning structure (from real LinkedIn data):**

Post 27 — 328 impressions, 1 comment (modal testing case study)
Post 28 — 383 impressions (GitHub library discovery, specific observation)
Post 20 — 2 likes, real consequence story

Both highest performing posts share the same structure:
```
Real situation that happened
    ↓
System insight (what it reveals)
    ↓
Constraint or lesson (what changed)
    ↓
Relatable framing (why you should care)
    ↓
[Optional] One-line CTA
```

**What FAILED (from real data):**
"Working on something around X… will share later." — 1–4 impressions consistently. Zero engagement. Killed by Content DNA rule.

**Content pillars for LinkedIn (with weights):**
- Technical systems & security (30%) — Ahmad's strongest niche, proven by data
- Founder decisions & lessons (25%) — contrarian takes, things that didn't work, honest assessments
- African tech perspective (20%) — building in Nigeria, payment friction, African developer experience
- Product building transparency (25%) — ColdSift, Oybit, Volari Finance — specific decisions and outcomes

**Posting cadence:** 5x/week minimum
**Optimal times (WAT):** 8–10AM weekdays, 12–1PM weekdays

**LinkedIn-specific rules:**
- Max 1,300 characters (LinkedIn's sweet spot — shorter performs better)
- No hashtag spam (max 3–5, relevant only)
- No images on every post — strong text posts outperform weak image posts
- Line breaks are essential — every 1–2 lines, break. Dense paragraphs get ignored.
- First line must be the hook — LinkedIn truncates at "...see more". If line 1 doesn't earn the click, the post is dead.
- Never start with "I" — algorithm penalizes self-referential openers

**Hook formulas that work for Ahmad's angle:**
- Contradiction: "The security scanner said it was clean. It wasn't."
- Consequence: "29 million secrets leaked on GitHub last year. Here's what that actually means."
- Counter-intuitive: "More automation doesn't mean more content. It means faster feedback loops."
- Specific number: "My automation SaaS processed 8,000 AI-generated lines yesterday."
- Personal incident: "2AM. The system drafted a LinkedIn post with my live Paystack key in it."

## Facebook Page (Nyvora)

**Goal:** 3,000–5,000 page followers by end of 2026
**Voice:** Mix of professional and accessible — this is the widest audience
**Core angle:** Nyvora as a company, with Ahmad as the visible founder

**Facebook reality:**
Facebook organic reach is difficult for pages. The algorithm heavily favors content that generates discussion (comments), content shared in groups, and video (especially live or native uploads). Facebook's strength is community and groups — not broadcast.

**Strategy:**
Facebook is primarily a **repurposing target** — LinkedIn posts, Instagram carousels, and Remotion videos repurposed with slight adaptation. It is not where original content is created first.

**Repurposing rules:**
- LinkedIn text posts → adapted for Facebook (slightly longer, more context, discussion question added at end)
- Instagram carousels → direct repost with caption adapted for Facebook audience
- Remotion videos → native video upload (never link to Instagram Reel — Facebook suppresses external links)

**Additional Facebook tactics:**
- Post in relevant Facebook Groups as Ahmad personally (not as Nyvora page) — genuine value, no spam
- Groups: Nigerian Developers, African Startup Communities, Developer groups
- Share Nyvora page posts to relevant groups when genuinely useful

**Content cadence:** 4x/week (lower priority than IG and LinkedIn)

## Platform Priority Order

For Ahmad right now, in order of strategic importance:

1. **LinkedIn** — highest ROI per post for professional credibility and follower quality. Ahmad's proven ground.
2. **Instagram Personal** — highest volume of daily content, trend adaptation, widest reach potential via Reels
3. **Instagram Brand** — company credibility and Nyvora visibility
4. **Facebook** — lowest priority, primarily repurposing channel

MiroFish allocates trend brief opportunities accordingly. If bandwidth is constrained, LinkedIn and Instagram Personal always get content first.

## Cross-Platform Content Flywheel

One piece of Ahmad's real activity → 4 platform-native posts:

```
Ahmad ships a feature at 2AM →

LinkedIn: "2AM. The system just blocked a post with a live key.
           Here's what I added to the pipeline."
           (technical storytelling, consequence, lesson)

Personal IG carousel: "What 2AM building actually looks like"
           (raw, relatable, behind-the-scenes angle)

Brand IG: Clean product visual: "Oybit's content guardian layer →
           protecting every post before it goes live"
           (product feature announcement, polished)

Facebook: LinkedIn post adapted with discussion question at end:
           "Has your automation ever done something it shouldn't have?
           What did you add to prevent it?"
```

One real event. Four different angles. All authentic. All platform-native. All generated by Oybit automatically once Ahmad's activity is detected or described in a brief.
